# Prompt: develop the SUST 720 Interactive Course Map

Copy everything below the line into Claude (or another AI tool). Attach your Course Map Template and Stakeholder Analysis Matrix PDFs.

---

You are helping a DIGI 230 team build Milestone 1: an **Interactive Course Map** of the SCAD course **SUST 720 Designing in Deep Time**. The map should be a visual page people can explore, not a written report or a slide deck.

**Course description:** Students study superforecasting, anti-fragility, world making, transformative scenario planning, speculative design and design fiction. They work on sustainability challenges across long time frames in co-creation workshops and rapid prototyping sessions.

**Bounded question:** How might graduate students studying sustainable design use algorithmic volatility modeling to stress-test anti-fragile systems while upholding human-only stewardship during 500-year scenario planning?

**The five course steps. Use these exactly, in order, from goal to assessment:**
1. **Ground.** Deep-time grounding through close reading and analog canvases.
   - Output: 500-Year Bioregional Baseline Canvas & Ontological Covenant.
   - Decider: Student Team.
   - AI: none. This is a non-use boundary.
2. **Stress-test.** Superforecasting and volatility modeling of compounding 500-year climate shocks (salinity inversion, permafrost thaw, biome migration lag).
   - Output: Four-Quadrant Volatility Matrix & Anti-Fragility Stress-Test Record.
   - Decider: Student Team.
   - AI: the course's only AI insertion point. The model proposes candidate compound shocks.
   - Hand-off: the Research Lead checks every shock against 2 peer-reviewed empirical climate papers. Faculty can strike any scenario. A shock that fails is logged as rejected.
3. **Architect.** Design a regenerative circular system and write the rules for AI use inside it.
   - Decider: Student Team, with a faculty check.
   - Human judgment must be preserved here.
4. **Co-create.** A live multi-stakeholder workshop and a physical prototype.
   - Deciders: Peer Teams and External Jurors.
   - AI: none. Trust between people can't be simulated.
5. **Charter.** Generational Ethics Charter & Deep-Time Anti-Fragility Blueprint.
   - Decider: Course Faculty grades with the SCAD graduate rubric.
   - Human judgment must be preserved here.

**Build a single self-contained HTML page with these five switchable layers:**
1. **Territory:** each step as a region, with its learning goal, activity, output and feedback.
2. **People:** the named roles in each region. These are the Student Team Research Lead, Student Team, Course Faculty, Peer Teams, External Jurors, and Future generations. Show future generations as having no voice.
3. **AI touchpoints:** mark what goes into the AI and what comes out. Mark at least one region where AI stays out, and say why.
4. **Hand-offs:** at every point where AI output meets human judgment, show who verifies it, who can override it, and what happens if it fails.
5. **Decision power:** who formally decides in each region, and who has informal influence.

**Show these three markers on the map:**
- AI insertion point
- Non-use boundary
- Preserve-human-judgment zone

**Rules for the page:**
- Text supports the map. Text does not lead. Keep to one idea per region, with no long text blocks.
- Pass the five-minute test. Without narration, a stranger should be able to answer two questions: "Where does AI touch this course?" and "Who does it touch?"
- Below the map, add these sections:
  - Make the Risk Visible: one concern, one benefit, one safeguard, and one unresolved question with a proposed test.
  - The stakeholder snapshot from the attached matrix.
  - Evidence cards. Each card needs these fields: source, stakeholders, benefit, concern, course implication, and an open question.
  - A Peer Trace Test checklist.
- Tag every claim as **evidence**, **assumption**, or **proposed test**.
- Use only sources you can verify. If you can't verify a source, flag it; never invent a citation.
- Make the page work on a phone, in light and dark mode, and when printed to PDF. The live link must appear on the first page of the PDF.
- The deliverable is `[TeamName]_M1_CourseMap.pdf` plus a live link that opens without a login.

**To develop the map further, suggest:**
- Stronger sources for each boundary.
- Sharper decision owners.
- Revisions after the Peer Trace Test.
